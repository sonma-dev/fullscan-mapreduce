## TP proof and generated correctness

The source-side overview atom removed during Phase 5 is retained as an explicit `REMOVED_OR_MERGED` audit entry. All linked actual physical Markdown evidence lines in those records were checked against mounted source files. These checks do **not** make the generated semantics independently certified: prior E2 bridges can be overbroad and generated-class reverse validity is a separate direction of entailment.

In the raw generated universe, **2,289** classes were retained; source-PDF support of **1,412** remains conservatively unresolved at the complete-qualifier level. They are all individually documented in `05_generated_reverse_evidence_audit_final.jsonl`. Of 2,289 retained classes, at least one PDF candidate passage was text-fragment-reconciled for 2,104; 185 candidate passages did not pass that literal text-fragment check (which can fail because of layout extraction and cannot by itself prove PDF absence). The final F=0 is an observed classification of conclusively proven unsupported canonical residuals in this bounded pass, **not** a finding that all generated claims are valid.
