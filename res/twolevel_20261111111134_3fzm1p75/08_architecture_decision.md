# 8. Architecture Decision Register

## How to Use This Document

This register consolidates the unresolved contracts, assumptions, alternatives and conflicts in the seven supplied Markdown specifications. It does not inspect or verify either PDF mentioned in those specifications, and it does not amend the completed files.

- **Confirmed requirements may guide implementation within their stated scope.** A confirmed reference-system constraint is not measured production performance or an approved production configuration.
- **TBD** means a necessary contract or selection has not been supplied.
- **Needs confirmation** means the supplied statements are incomplete, ambiguous or conflicting.
- **Unadopted proposal** covers an optional design alternative that has not been selected. It does not imply that this register recommends it.
- A missing detail, repeated assertion, illustrative configuration or later-generated statement does not authorize an implementation AI to choose independently.
- **Resolved** applies only to the specific question answered explicitly and consistently. Related schemas, mechanisms and operational choices can remain open.

Quotes below are quotations from the supplied Markdown specifications, not independently verified source quotations. Requirement and open-question identifiers retain their original spelling. Some specifications reuse or group open IDs; the subject and section distinguish those references.
